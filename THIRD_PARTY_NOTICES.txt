IV Switchboard reads the usage records that Claude Code, Codex and Hermes already keep on
your PC. It is not made by or affiliated with Anthropic, OpenAI, xAI or Nous Research. Prices
shown are each provider's published API prices, for comparison only; they are not what
your subscription costs.

The idea came from codex-switch (https://github.com/joshp123/codex-switch). No code was
copied from it, and IV Switchboard does not pool or switch accounts.
